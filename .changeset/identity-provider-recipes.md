Recipes for identity providers' preview URLs: `auth0.callback_url`, `auth0.logout_url`, `auth0.web_origin` and `auth0.allowed_origin` (Management API, client-credentials token), `firebase.authorized_domain` (Identity Toolkit, `gcloud auth print-access-token`), `okta.redirect_uri`, `okta.post_logout_redirect_uri` and `okta.trusted_origin` (`SSWS` API token), and `kinde.callback_url` and `kinde.logout_url` (Management API, client-credentials token).
